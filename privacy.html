Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

This policy covers the DogparkPack Uploader, an internal tool operated by Jannis Tjark Folger, Germany (contact: doggo_en.capricorn232@passmail.net). The tool is used only to publish our own videos to our own social media accounts. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

The DogparkPack Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the account owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the tool accesses

Only after the channel owner signs in with Google (OAuth) and grants access, the tool:

  • uploads videos chosen by the owner to the owner's YouTube channel (scope youtube.upload);
  • reads the channel's id, name and subscriber count, and the view, like and comment counts of the videos the tool uploaded (scope youtube.readonly).

It does not read comments, messages, watch history, or any data about viewers or other channels.

How the data is used, stored and shared

  • Data is used only to schedule our own uploads and to show us how our own videos perform.
  • OAuth tokens, video ids and statistics are stored locally on the operator's own computer. Nothing is stored on a public server.
  • Data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

  • Statistics are refreshed at least every 30 days and are deleted when they are older than allowed by the YouTube API Services policies.
  • When a channel is disconnected in the tool, its tokens and stored data are deleted.
  • Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page. To ask for deletion of any stored data, write to the contact address above; we delete it within 7 days.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers.

Changes

We will update this page if the tool's use of data changes, with a new date at the top.
